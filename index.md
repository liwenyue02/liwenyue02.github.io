## About Me

I am an AI Application Developer at the Digital Intelligence Department of [Hubei Yihua Group Co., Ltd.](https://www.hbyihua.cn/index.html). I completed my M.Sc. in Applied Statistics at the [School of Mathematics and Statistics, Chongqing University](https://sci.cqu.edu.cn/) in 2025. During my studies, I received the National Scholarship at both the undergraduate and graduate levels, the Category A Academic Scholarship, and the university-level "Outstanding Student" award, and I am a certified National Statistician.

**My work focuses on applying large language models and graph-based methods to recommendation and social network analysis, with hands-on experience in product monitoring, user analytics, data mining, and strategy optimization.**

I have interned in business analytics at Beijing Maoyan Entertainment (Meituan Film), data analysis at the Innovation Academy for Precision Measurement Science and Technology, data mining at Wuhan Taidi, and quantitative strategy research at Shenzhen DigQuant. I have participated in three research projects funded by the Chongqing Municipal Bureau of Statistics and led a provincial innovation training project in Hubei. My research has appeared in venues including ACM MM Asia and *Cybersecurity*, and I hold a utility model patent as the first inventor.
